# Veeenz

Welcome to **Veeenz**! An engaging game where your goal is to touch the runner during its stops at each alignment. Progress through levels and tackle increasingly challenging tasks before time runs out!

## Features

- **Runner Movement**: The runner moves according to various patterns, including circular, Z-shaped, and N-shaped movements.
- **Level Objectives**: Each level has a specific target to reach in order to advance.
- **Power Management**: Use your powers to gain extra time and enhance your performance.
- **Increasing Difficulty**: The movement duration decreases as you progress through the levels, increasing the challenge.
- **Scoring System**: Keep track of your progress and scores across levels.

## How to Play

1. **Start the Game**: When the game begins, the runner moves according to a predefined pattern.
2. **Touch the Runner**: Tap the runner when it stops at an alignment.
3. **Advance through Levels**: Reach the level target before the time runs out to progress to the next level.
4. **Use Powers**: If you have powers, tap the screen to add 10 extra seconds to the timer.
